### [1] [AV-GRPO: Modality-Anchored Decoupling Diffusion Reinforcement Learning for Joint Audio-Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29816)
*Zhiyu Xu,Weilong Yan,Yufei Shi,Shiyang Li,Yihao Liu,Kin-Man Lam,Yuewen Cao*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出 AV-GRPO 模态锚定的在线扩散强化学习框架与 5DAV 数据集，将耦合的多模态偏好学习解耦为单模态子问题，提升音视频联合生成质量、语义对齐与跨模态同步。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有音视频联合生成模型存在单模态保真度有限、文本-模态对齐不足、跨模态同步弱的问题。直接引入强化学习后训练面临挑战：异构多模态奖励纠缠学习信号、信用分配困难；双模态塔联合优化计算昂贵且动态差异大；同步评估依赖配对样本，难以公平比较奖励。

Method: 提出 AV-GRPO（模态锚定的在线扩散 RL 框架），含三模块：(1) 模态锚定 rollout 解耦学习信号并稳定难度；(2) 轨迹锁定冻结塔优化降低成本、重分配信用；(3) 针对模态动态的自适应目标与扰动强度。同时构建 5DAV 解耦、难度可控的训练数据集，沿五个维度解耦样本。

Result: 在 JavisBench 与 VABench 上，AV-GRPO 在 LoRA 和全量微调下均于生成质量、语义对齐、跨模态同步方面超越 LTX-2.3。消融实验验证了各设计的有效性。

Conclusion: 将耦合的多模态偏好学习转化为单模态子问题，可实现精确奖励归因并改善同步，为音视频联合生成提供有效的强化学习后训练方案。

Abstract: Recent years have witnessed major progress in joint audio-video generation. Existing models still suffer from limited per-modality fidelity, insufficient text-modality alignment and weak cross-modal synchronization. While reinforcement-learning post-training offers a promising remedy, directly adapting it to joint audio-video generation is challenging. Heterogeneous multimodal rewards entangle learning signals and complicate credit assignment. Joint optimization of two modality towers is computationally expensive given their divergent dynamics. Moreover, synchronization evaluation difficulty depends on paired samples, preventing fair reward comparisons. We propose AV-GRPO, a modality-anchored online diffusion RL framework, and 5DAV, a decoupled, difficulty-controllable training dataset. AV-GRPO includes three key modules: (1) modality-anchored rollouts to disentangle learning signals and stabilize difficulty; (2) trajectory-locked frozen-tower optimization to reduce cost and reassign credit; (3) adaptive objectives and perturbation strengths tailored to modality-specific dynamics. This converts coupled multimodal preference learning into unimodal subproblems for precise reward attribution and better synchronization. Our 5DAV dataset decouples samples across five dimensions for systematic training. Experiments on JavisBench and VABench demonstrate AV-GRPO outperforms LTX-2.3 in generation quality, semantic alignment and cross-modal synchronization under LoRA and full fine-tuning. Ablations confirm our designs. Code and data: https://github.com/zhiyuxu03/AV-GRPO

### [2] [MeshHeal: Two-Timescale Self-Healing for Gray Failures in Decentralized LLM Agent Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29015)
*Keru Chen,Sen Lin,Yingbin Liang,Nathaniel D. Bastian,Shaofeng Zou*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: MeshHeal是一个去中心化LLM多智能体自愈框架，通过双时间尺度的能力匹配同行评审检测并隔离“灰色故障”智能体，并在恢复后重新纳入。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 去中心化多智能体系统中，智能体可能仍保持响应但任务解决质量持续下降（灰色故障），需在证据不足时保护当前任务，同时允许恢复后的智能体重新加入。

Method: 快速时间尺度：自适应层级将不确定或低分输出从单评审升级到委员会审议及纠正；慢速时间尺度：任务与能力条件下的同行相对检测器聚合分数，区分持续退化与普通波动，触发强制委员会审查并最终排除退化智能体，恢复探测提供重新纳入证据。并提出Model-Backed MAS Evaluation，将能力分配绑定到执行模型。

Result: 在BBH、MATH、MMLU-Pro上，MeshHeal退化阶段准确率0.839，每任务51k模型token；最强基线Symphony为0.807准确率，每任务115k token。在交错退化与恢复下，能隔离退化智能体并保持排除直至恢复，随后恢复正常路由。

Conclusion: MeshHeal有效实现去中心化自愈，在更高效率下优于基线，并能动态处理智能体退化与恢复。

Abstract: Decentralized LLM-based multi-agent systems coordinate through local interactions, but an agent can remain responsive while its task-solving quality persistently degrades. Such gray failures require protecting current tasks before sufficient evidence exists to alter future routing, while still allowing recovered agents to rejoin. We introduce MeshHeal, a fully decentralized self-healing framework that couples ability-matched peer review across two timescales. At the fast timescale, an adaptive hierarchy escalates uncertain or low-scoring outputs from repeated single-reviewer evaluation to committee deliberation and, when needed, correction before use. At the slow timescale, a task- and ability-conditioned peer-relative detector aggregates scores to distinguish persistent degradation from ordinary output variation, trigger mandatory committee review, and eventually exclude degraded agents from ordinary routing; recovery probes provide fresh evidence for reintegration. To faithfully evaluate routing, we introduce Model-Backed MAS Evaluation, which ties ability assignments to execution models, since prompt-based ability assignments alone can leave routing errors hidden. Across BBH, MATH, and MMLU-Pro, MeshHeal achieves 0.839 degraded-phase accuracy using 51k total model tokens per task, versus the strongest baseline Symphony's 0.807 accuracy using 115k per task. Under staggered degradation and recovery, MeshHeal isolates degraded agents, keeps them excluded from ordinary task execution until recovery, and returns them to normal routing.

### [3] [Evaluation of Multi-Turn Consistency in LLM Agents: Survival Analysis and Failure-Rationale Taxonomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29508)
*Igor Bogdanov,Olga Manakina,Chung-Horng Lung*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 该论文在20步多智能体延迟满足实验中评估LLM智能体的时间一致性，通过生存分析与理由分类揭示失败的时间规律与模型特征。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM智能体在孤立任务上表现良好，但在长时交互中可能逐渐出现不一致，缺乏对多轮行为时间一致性的系统评估方法。

Method: 设计受延迟满足研究启发的20步多智能体场景，智能体每步选择延迟奖励或立即领取（终止）。全因子操控社会可见性、人格压力源与审议策略，运行84,540条轨迹、覆盖8个模型家族。以首次领取奖励为事件结局，估计Kaplan-Meier生存曲线并拟合离散时间风险回归；并对13,780条审议痕迹构建七类理由分类法（LLM辅助标注+人工审核，κ=0.83）。

Result: 失败理由随时间与情境系统变化：早期失败更冲动，后期失败更多疲劳与成本收益框架，公开情境增加规范导向理由。失败者中更长的审议与更高的理由内部矛盾率相关（同时出现支持延迟与支持领取的表述），挑战"推理文本越多越一致"的假设。

Conclusion: 生存与理由分析共同揭示出不同的时间可靠性阶段及模型特有的"失败指纹"，为诊断多轮智能体行为不一致提供了评估视角。

Abstract: Large language model (LLM) agents may perform well on isolated tasks yet drift into inconsistency over extended interaction. We evaluate temporal consistency in a controlled 20-step multi-agent setting inspired by delayed-gratification studies. At each step, an agent chooses between continuing to delay a reward or claiming it immediately (terminating the episode). Across a full-factorial manipulation of social visibility (private vs public), persona stressors, and deliberation policy, we run 84,540 trajectories spanning 8 model families. Treating the first reward-claim as a time-to-event outcome, we estimate Kaplan-Meier survival curves and fit discrete-time hazard regression to quantify how experimental factors shift failure risk over time. Then, to analyze rationales and language patterns associated with failure, we build a seven-category taxonomy from 13,780 deliberation traces from agents who choose to terminate the episode, using an LLM-assisted labeling paired with human audit ($κ=0.83$). Rationale profiles change systematically with time and context: early failures are more impulse-driven, later failures more fatigue- and cost-benefit-framed, while public settings increase norm-oriented justifications. We also find a deliberation-inconsistency association: among failures, longer deliberation correlates with higher rates of intra-rationale contradiction (simultaneous pro-delay and pro-claim statements), challenging the assumption that more reasoning text implies greater consistency. Together, the survival and rationale analyses reveal distinct temporal reliability regimes and model-specific "failure fingerprints", offering an evaluation lens for diagnosing inconsistency in multi-turn agent behavior.

### [4] [Forecast-Dojo: Replayable Environments for Benchmarking and Training LLM Forecasting Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28876)
*Liqin Ye,Haorui Wang,Fardin Ahmed,Rongzhi Zhang,Yuan He,Ziyuan Lin,Yanbin Yin,Jing Peng,Michael Galarnyk,Sudheer Chava,Chao Zhang*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: Forecast-Dojo：一个可重放环境，用已结算预测市场问题+带日期新闻来评测和训练LLM预测智能体，含1,568个事件与18.8M新闻。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 预测智能体需要可重复评估、能收集训练交互并获取结果反馈的环境，而真实事件结算周期长、无法快速重放，现有基准难以支撑评测与训练闭环。

Method: 将已结算的Polymarket事件与带日期新闻配对，按时间划分训练/评估期，允许智能体在研究事件后于不同历史日期重访预测；同一任务与工具支持重复评测、轨迹收集和基于记录结果的反馈。

Result: 12个模型评测中，研究工具降低全部模型的Brier分数；随事件展开预测改善，新证据多的步骤增益最大；所有模型在Brier分数与准确率上仍落后于历史市场预测；跨日期携带的信念笔记本降低研究成本但不稳定提升质量。

Conclusion: Forecast-Dojo既可做评测，也提供交互轨迹与结果反馈用于智能体学习，并以监督微调作为概念验证。

Abstract: We introduce Forecast-Dojo, a replayable environment for benchmarking and training LLM forecasting agents. It combines resolved prediction-market questions with dated news, allowing agents to research an event and revisit their predictions at successive historical dates. The same tasks and tools support repeated evaluation, collection of training interactions, and feedback from recorded outcomes without waiting for new events to resolve. Forecast-Dojo contains 1,568 Polymarket events, split by time into training and evaluation periods, and 18.8M dated news articles. In an evaluation of 12 models, research tools lower Brier score for all 12. Forecasts also improve as events unfold, with the largest gains at steps where more newly dated evidence is recorded. Every model still trails historical market forecasts in both Brier score and accuracy. A belief notebook carried between dates lowers research cost but does not consistently improve forecast quality. Beyond evaluation, Forecast-Dojo provides interaction trajectories and outcome feedback for agent learning, with supervised fine-tuning as a proof of concept.

### [5] [An Empirical Study of VLM Pipelines for Long-Document QA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29933)
*Kenan E. Ak,Jay Mohta,Gwang Gook Lee,Yan Xu,Dimitrios Dimitriadis*

Main category: cs.CL

TL;DR: 论文系统研究长文档VLM QA中的三大部署选择（输入喂给方式、检索模态、agentic vs 静态pipeline），发现在MMLongBench-Doc和LongDocURL上，agentic工具调用的收益依赖VLM规模；图像检索模态优于文本；不同最优pipeline互补性强，oracle可提升约13点。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 长文档处理中VLM输入包含文本、图表、表格和复杂版式，部署时需决定如何喂文档、用哪种检索器、是否用agentic方式。这些选择缺乏系统性研究。

Method: 在两个长文档QA基准（MMLongBench-Doc、LongDocURL）上，使用前沿API和开源VLM，对比六工具agent（页/表/图/搜索调用）与静态页输入；比较图像vs文本检索模态及不同检索器；分析多pipeline互补性并尝试证据类型路由。

Result: ①agentic收益随VLM规模出现：Qwen3.5-4B/9B落后静态输入，27B持平，Sonnet 4.5领先；LongDocURL上各reader持平或领先。②检索模态比具体检索器更重要：最强图像检索器胜过最强文本pipeline，单个cross-encoder rerank即可匹敌多阶段LLM pipeline；top-k图像检索最省token（约为全页的1/7~1/4）。③最强的三个pipeline在不同问题上成功，oracle每问题选最优可提升约13点，但证据类型路由几乎无法复现该收益。

Conclusion: 长文档VLM部署中，agentic与检索模态的选择需结合reader能力权衡；pipeline之间存在显著互补性但目前缺乏有效的路由机制来利用，是未来方向。

Abstract: Vision-Language Models (VLMs) are increasingly used for long-document processing, where the inputs combine text with charts, tables, figures, and complex layouts. Deploying them means choosing how to feed the document to the model, which retriever to use when only a subset of pages is sent, and whether to run the model agentically or as a static pipeline. We study these choices on two long-document QA benchmarks with both frontier API and open-weight VLMs. First, on MMLongBench-Doc our six-tool agent with page, table, figure, and search calls pays off only once the answering VLM is large enough: with Qwen3.5-4B and 9B it trails static page input, with Qwen3.5-27B it draws level, and with Sonnet 4.5 it leads. On LongDocURL it is level with or ahead of static input at every reader. Its lead over the strongest static pipeline is clearest with the frontier reader on MMLongBench-Doc and narrows to within noise on LongDocURL. Second, retrieval modality matters more than the specific retriever: the strongest image retriever leads the strongest text pipeline, and on the text side a single off-the-shelf cross-encoder rerank essentially matches a much heavier multi-stage LLM pipeline. Top-k image retrieval is also the most token-efficient input at every reader we paired it with, at roughly a seventh to a quarter of the tokens of sending every page. Third, cutting across all three choices, three of our strongest pipelines succeed on different questions, and an oracle that picks the best pipeline per question gains roughly thirteen points over the best single pipeline, though evidence-type routing recovers almost none of it.

### [6] [Reasoning Instructions Can Break Answer Decoding in Vision--Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29278)
*Zeyan Li,Siyuan Qiu,Jianfeng Xu*

Main category: cs.CL

TL;DR: 本文指出"CoT前缀评分"（在生成理由前读取答案标签logits）会扭曲多选VLM评测，尤其是位置偏置，并提出应避免该评分方式。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当评分器在答案标签logits上打分却先附加推理提示（CoT指令）时，评测可能将模型知识与评测接口不匹配混淆，需要识别这一问题的严重性。

Method: 在ScienceQA等数据集上，对Qwen2.5-VL-7B等模型进行CoT前缀评分实验，并对比条件匹配线性探针与自由生成；通过词表和层诊断分析预测偏移原因。

Result: Qwen2.5-VL-7B在ScienceQA上从80.76%降至45.48%；五种选项排列中93.54%的CoT前缀预测选择第一项；线性探针恢复78.94%，自由生成恢复75.24%；诊断显示概率质量转向续写token，而答案信息在后期层仍线性可达。

Conclusion: CoT前缀评分会将模型知识与评测接口不匹配相混淆，除非所请求与所评分的输出事件对齐，否则应避免使用。

Abstract: Chain-of-thought (CoT) instructions can distort multiple-choice VLM evaluation when a scorer appends a reasoning cue but reads answer-label logits before the model generates any rationale. We call this CoT-prefix scoring. On ScienceQA, Qwen2.5-VL-7B drops from 80.76% to 45.48%, and across five option-content permutations 93.54% of CoT-prefix predictions select the first slot. Condition-matched linear probes recover 78.94% from the same hidden states, while free generation restores 75.24%, showing that the answer often survives the prefix and the immediate readout fails. Vocabulary and layer diagnostics explain the mismatch: probability mass moves toward continuation tokens, while answer information remains linearly accessible in late layers. The effect recurs with varying severity across datasets and models, though not universally. These results show that CoT-prefix scoring can confound model knowledge with an evaluation-interface mismatch and should be avoided unless the requested and scored output events are aligned.

</details>
