TL;DR: 本文提出一种以事件为中心的视频生成框架，将物理演化表示为因果连接的事件链，通过物理驱动的推理、键帧引导和对比语义引导三个模块，生成符合物理规律的视频。

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 本文提出三个关键模块：(1) 物理驱动的事件链推理：将物理现象分解为因果事件，用演化场景图表示，并将公式推导的物理量绑定到对象和交互上；(2) 过渡感知的路由键帧条件化：把每个事件路由到专门的键帧合成算子处理外观变化或对象变换，并将连续键帧作为去噪过程中的残差引导以实现平滑视觉过渡；(3) 物理注入的对比语义引导：构造物理正例和反事实负例提示用于无分类器引导，推动生成向合理动力学方向靠拢。

Experiments on PhyGenBench, VideoPhy, PhyWorldBench, and Physics-IQ demonstrate that our framework generates videos with superior physical plausibility across diverse domains.

Result: 通过在包含洋流和风环境的物理引擎仿真中，对多个无人水面艇代理进行了系统演示，验证了SCARAB在复杂环境条件下的自主定位、编队和航行的可行性。

Conclusion: SCARAB系统有望为陆军提供不可预测的分散式渡河能力，提升乘员生存性并减少后勤足迹，同时其无GPS/RF依赖特性也使其可应用于民用救灾等非对抗场景。

Abstract: We describe SCARAB--Swarm-Capable Autonomous Robotic Aquatic Bridging. Using distributed swarm control and multi-model sensing of agents and docking targets, agents can localize themselves, join into formations and proceed to desired target locations. Our technologies would eventually allow the Army to perform unpredictable, dispersed river crossings, enhance crew survivability, and minimize the logistics footprint compared to the current Improved Ribbon Bridge. Our methods operate without GPS or RF communications, though these can be used in non-contested environments (e.g., civilian disaster relief for flooding, etc.). We demonstrate our system via physics-engine-based simulation of several agents using unmanned surface vehicles (USVs) in the presence of currents and wind.
